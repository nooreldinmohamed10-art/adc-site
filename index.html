About the centre

Advanced care,
delivered gently.

Advanced Dental Centre was founded on the idea that good dentistry is both precise and patient. Under Dr. Mostafa Nassif, the team takes the time to explain every step — especially for younger patients taking their first steps into dental care.

Every treatment plan is built around comfort, modern equipment and honest advice, for the whole family.

Every patient, treated gently — no comparisons, no rush.
